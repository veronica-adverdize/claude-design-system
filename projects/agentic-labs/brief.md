# Agentic Labs — Brand Brief

**Business:** AI agent staffing service for Singapore SMEs
**Location:** Singapore
**Domain:** agenticai.com.sg

## Audience
SME owners and operators (construction, renovation, accountancy, legal, F&B, clinics, logistics, tuition, aircon, property, professional services). Non-technical. Time-poor. Spending evenings on admin they can't hire for.

## Proposition
Seven named AI agents handle quotes, scheduling, chasing, follow-ups, and reporting — and check with the owner before sending anything. Agentic Labs builds, runs, and maintains them. Nothing to install, no technical knowledge required.

## Voice
Plain English. Direct. Singapore-contextual. Non-technical. Conversational without being casual. "They handle the admin. You make the decisions."

## Key content (supplied)
- **Agents:** Nigel (orchestrator), Ben (intake), Chloe (planning), Tiffany (scheduling), Sarah (coordination), Priya (risk), Haziq (reporting)
- **Problems:** Evening admin pile, quotes that never left, follow-ups that didn't happen
- **Differentiator:** Not a chatbot (waits for you), not DIY automation (breaks on exceptions). Runs automatically, knows your business, handles exceptions, asks before sending.
- **Workflow:** Job done → write-up → threshold check → sent & tracked → follow-up
- **Departments:** Finance, Sales, Operations, Customer Service, Marketing, Staff Admin
- **Pricing:** $1,500/mo (1 agent), $3,000/mo (up to 3), $5,000/mo (up to 10). 6-month minimum, annual saves 20%.
- **Industries:** 12+ named, "all 30 industries" mentioned
- **Trust signals:** Singapore-based, nothing to install, no technical knowledge needed
- **CTA:** Book a free 30-minute call

## Existing identity
None confirmed. Previous directions used Instrument Serif + Inter, and Cabinet Grotesk. No logo, no photography, no brand colours established.

## Content gaps
- No photography (no team, no office, no client imagery)
- No real testimonials
- No case studies or named clients
- No specific results or metrics (all illustrative)
- Dashboard UI is conceptual — must be labeled clearly

## Constraints
- All dashboard data, agent activity, names, and figures are illustrative
- No credentials, results, or testimonials to invent
- Pricing is supplied and may be used as given

## Section argument

Claims with proof, in page order (sample scope: first three only):

1. AI agents that handle your admin and ask before acting. → The morning review dashboard: a short list of items needing sign-off, everything else already done. The dashboard is the proof because the product has no other visual.
2. The specific admin work that never gets done. → Three named problems the audience recognises from their own evenings: the admin pile, the unsent quote, the missed follow-up. Proof is recognition, not metrics.
3. How the daily approval works. → An annotated review showing a quote above threshold, a follow-up draft, and overnight completions. The owner taps approve or edits — that's the whole interaction. Proof is the simplicity of the interface.

Sections without proof (deferred to full site):
- Pricing: supplied, can be used as given — but no proof that it's good value (no case studies or ROI).
- Departments/industries: listed but no evidence of sector-specific knowledge.
- Agent profiles: names supplied but no demonstrated track record.
